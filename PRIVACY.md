# Privacy

The app has no analytics, no ads, no trackers and no Google services.

**What it sends to the Chord-A-Licious server (https://chordalicious.co.nz):**
- Your e-mail address and password, to log in (the app never stores the password; it keeps the server's session cookie).
- Each audio file you open, or recording you make, so the server can work out its chords. The server keeps the analysis for the time it takes to play the song; a copy of the audio is kept only when you save the song to Favourites.
- The Favourites, chord templates and print counts you create, tied to your account.
- A header saying the request came from the Android app (used for aggregate usage counts).

**What stays on the phone:** the recents list for the current session, and any PDF you save (to the place you choose).
**Permissions:** Internet (to reach the server) and Microphone (only when you press Record yourself).
**Payments:** Pro is bought on the website in your phone's browser through Stripe. The app never sees card details.

The server's own privacy policy is at https://chordalicious.co.nz/privacy
